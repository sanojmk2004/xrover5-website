If you have opened a trading app and scrolled past currency pairs like EURUSD and GBPUSD, you have probably seen XAUUSD near the top of the most-traded list. It is gold, priced in US dollars, and it is one of the most popular instruments among retail traders in India and around the world. This guide explains what XAUUSD is, how it works and what a new trader should understand before placing a first trade.

What does XAUUSD mean?

Trading symbols follow the international ISO currency code format. XAU is the code for one troy ounce of gold ("Au" is gold's chemical symbol) and USD is the US dollar. So XAUUSD shows how many US dollars it costs to buy one troy ounce of gold.

If the chart shows XAUUSD at 4,000.00, one ounce of gold costs 4,000 US dollars. When the price rises, gold is getting more expensive in dollar terms; when it falls, gold is getting cheaper.

Troy ounce, not regular ounce: a troy ounce is about 31.1 grams, slightly heavier than the everyday ounce. All international gold prices use it.

How you actually trade it

Most retail traders don't buy physical gold bars when they trade XAUUSD. They trade a CFD (contract for difference) or a spot contract through a broker. You never take delivery of metal; you simply profit or lose on the change in price between your entry and exit.

  • Buy (go long) if you expect the gold price to rise.
  • Sell (go short) if you expect it to fall. Being able to profit from falling prices is a key difference from buying jewellery or coins.
  • Trades are sized in lots. On most platforms, 1 standard lot = 100 ounces, and you can trade as small as 0.01 lot (1 ounce).

Why is XAUUSD so popular?

1. It moves

Gold regularly moves tens of dollars in a single day, and much more around major news. For short-term traders, movement means opportunity. It also means risk, which is why position sizing matters so much with gold.

2. Deep liquidity

Gold is traded by central banks, funds, jewellers, miners and millions of individual traders. That depth usually means tight spreads during active hours and easy entry and exit.

3. Nearly 24-hour trading

XAUUSD trades from Monday morning to late Friday night (Indian time), following the Asian, London and New York sessions. Working professionals can trade in the evening, which happens to be when gold is often most active. See the best times to trade gold in IST.

4. It responds to big, understandable themes

Interest rates, inflation, the US dollar and global uncertainty all influence gold. Once you understand what moves the gold price, the charts start to make a lot more sense.

The risks you must respect

The same qualities that make gold attractive also make it dangerous for unprepared traders:

  • Volatility cuts both ways. A move that would give a profit on a correct trade gives an equal loss on a wrong one.
  • Leverage magnifies results. Brokers let you control a large position with a small deposit. Small price moves can create large percentage gains or losses on your account.
  • News spikes. Around US data releases, gold can jump sharply in seconds, spreads can widen and stop-losses can fill at worse prices than planned.
