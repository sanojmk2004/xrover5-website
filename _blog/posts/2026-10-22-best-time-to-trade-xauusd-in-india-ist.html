Gold trades almost around the clock, but it does not move the same way all day. Some hours are quiet and choppy; others bring strong, clean moves. For traders in India, knowing these windows in IST is one of the simplest ways to improve your trading.

The three main sessions

XAUUSD follows the global financial day as it passes through three major centres. Times below are approximate and in IST. Because the UK and US change their clocks for daylight saving and India does not, session times shift by one hour twice a year.

Clock changes: the UK moves its clocks on the last Sunday of March and October, and the US on the second Sunday of March and the first Sunday of November. For a week or two each spring and autumn the gap between London and New York is different from usual, so check your broker's server time.

1. The London–New York overlap: prime time for gold

For most of the year, the window from the New York open until a few hours later is the most active period for XAUUSD. Both of the world's largest trading centres are open, liquidity is deep and spreads are usually at their tightest. This is also when key US economic data is released.

The good news for working traders in India is that this window falls in the evening, after office hours.

2. The London open: the first big push

Around early afternoon IST, European traders arrive. Gold often breaks out of the range it built during the Asian session. Some traders specialise only in this breakout window.

3. The Asian session: quieter, but not useless

Morning hours in India are usually calmer for gold. Moves tend to be smaller and price often moves sideways. That suits range-style trading but can frustrate breakout traders. Exceptions happen when major news from China or overnight geopolitical events hits the market.

Hours to be careful with

High-impact US news releases

Most important US data, including CPI inflation, Non-Farm Payrolls and weekly jobless claims, is published at 8:30 am New York time, about 18:00 IST in summer and 19:00 IST in winter. Fed interest rate decisions usually come at 2:00 pm New York time, around 23:30 IST in summer or 00:30 IST in winter. In the minutes around these releases, gold can spike in both directions and spreads widen. Many traders deliberately stay flat until the dust settles.

The daily rollover and the Monday open

Around the New York close (late night or early morning IST, depending on the season), most brokers have a short break in gold trading. Spreads around this time are often much wider. The Monday open can also gap away from Friday's close after weekend news.

Late Friday

Liquidity fades as the week ends. Moves can be erratic and you may be holding positions over a weekend where you can't react.

Build your routine around your session

  • Choose one or two windows you can trade consistently and focus on them instead of watching charts all day.
  • Check the economic calendar every morning for high-impact USD events.
  • Track your results by session. Many traders find they perform much better in one window than another.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to trade gold in India?

For most of the year, the London–New York overlap is the most active time for XAUUSD. It runs from about 17:30 to 21:30 IST in summer (12:00–16:00 GMT) and about 18:30 to 22:30 IST in winter (13:00–17:00 GMT).

Why do gold session times change during the year?

The UK and the US change their clocks for daylight saving, but India does not. So the London and New York sessions start about one hour later in IST during the northern winter.

When is US news released in IST?

Most major US data, such as CPI and Non-Farm Payrolls, is released at 8:30 am New York time. That is about 18:00 IST in summer and 19:00 IST in winter.

Which hours should gold traders be careful with?

Be careful in the minutes around high-impact US news, around the daily rollover near the New York close, at the Monday open and late on Friday, when spreads can widen and moves can be erratic.
