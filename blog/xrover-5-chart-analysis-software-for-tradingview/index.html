Inside XRover 5: How Our Intelligent Candle System Works on TradingView

Product GuideBy XRover Tech1 November 20263 min read

XRover 5 is chart-analysis software that runs inside TradingView. We call it an Intelligent Candle System because everything it shows is built from one source: the candlestick data on the chart, analysed as each new candle forms. This article explains what the software does, how it is delivered and how it is designed.

Built on candle data

Every candlestick on a chart records four prices for its period: open, high, low and close. Together, candles show how activity built up, paused and changed over time. Reading them by eye across sessions and timeframes is slow and inconsistent. XRover 5 does that reading continuously, using fixed rules, and draws its analysis directly on the chart.

Key features

Real-time candle analysis

XRover 5 updates as new candles form, so what you see on the chart reflects the latest data rather than a snapshot from earlier in the day.

Adaptive zones

Instead of fixed lines drawn once and forgotten, XRover 5 maps zones that adjust as new candles arrive. When conditions change, the zones change with them.

Two display modes

  • Smart Mode keeps the chart simple, showing only the essentials. It is designed for users who are new to the software.
  • Professional Mode shows more detail and more configuration for experienced users.

On-chart dashboard and template library

A compact dashboard summarises the current state of the analysis in one place. A built-in template library lets users start from ready-made configurations and adjust them to their own preferences and schedule.

Countdowns and session timers

Timers show when the next candle-based event is due and which market session is active, so users know when to look at the chart instead of watching it all day.

Alerts on TradingView and Telegram

XRover 5 uses TradingView's alert system, and alerts can also be delivered to Telegram, so users are notified on their phone when the chart needs attention.

How XRover 5 is delivered

XRover 5 is published on TradingView as an invite-only script. After a user subscribes, we grant access to their TradingView username. The script then appears in the "Invite-only scripts" section of TradingView's Indicators menu, and the user adds it to any chart like a normal indicator. There is nothing to download or install.

Design principles

  • Rules, not guesswork. The same candle data always produces the same analysis.
  • Clarity first. The chart should become easier to read, not more crowded. That is why Smart Mode exists.
  • Notifications that respect your time. Countdowns and Telegram alerts mean users do not have to stare at the screen.
  • Continuous improvement. XRover 5 is actively developed by XRover Tech, with new versions released as the software improves.
Analysis software only. XRover 5 analyses chart data and displays it. It does not give advice, hold funds or place orders, and users make their own decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What is XRover 5?

XRover 5 is chart-analysis software from XRover Tech that runs on TradingView. It analyses candlestick data in real time and shows adaptive zones, a dashboard, timers and alerts on the chart.

How do I add XRover 5 to TradingView?

XRover 5 is an invite-only script. After access is granted to your TradingView username, open Indicators, choose Invite-only scripts and add XRover 5 to your chart.

What is the difference between Smart Mode and Professional Mode?

Smart Mode shows a simplified chart with only the essentials, while Professional Mode shows more detail and more settings for experienced users.

Can XRover 5 send alerts to my phone?

Yes. XRover 5 works with TradingView alerts, and alerts can also be delivered to Telegram.
